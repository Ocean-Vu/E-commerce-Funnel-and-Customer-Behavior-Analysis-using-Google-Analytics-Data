# E-commerce-Funnel-and-Customer-Behavior-Analysis-using-Google-Analytics-Data
This project analyzes the public Google Analytics dataset for the Google Merchandise Store to uncover key business insights. Using SQL in BigQuery, I investigate customer behavior, traffic source effectiveness, and the purchasing funnel so that I identify trends and provide data-driven recommendations to enhance user engagement and drive sales
## Dataset 
The analysis utilizes the public Google Analytics sample dataset available on Google BigQuery.

**- Source:** bigquery-public-data.google_analytics_sample.ga_sessions_*

**- Timeframe:** 12 months of data from August 1, 2016, to August 1, 2017.

**- Content:** The dataset is deeply nested and contains rich information typical of an e-commerce website, including:

  **+ Traffic Source Data:** The origin of visitors (e.g., Organic, Paid Search, Social).

  **+ Content & Behavior Data:** Pages viewed, events triggered, and on-site user interactions.

  **+ Transactional Data:** Detailed information about purchases, products, and revenue.

## Business Questions & Analytical Strategy (Câu hỏi kinh doanh & Chiến lược phân tích)
The analysis is structured to answer key business questions by exploring several strategic areas:

**- Website Performance Metrics:** Evaluating core KPIs like visits, pageviews, and transactions to gauge overall site health and user engagement (Query 01).

**- Traffic Source Analysis:** Identifying the most effective channels by analyzing bounce rates and revenue generated by each traffic source (Query 02, 03).

**- Purchaser Behavior Analysis:** Understanding the distinct behaviors of purchasers versus non-purchasers, including their engagement levels, transaction frequency, and average spending (Query 04, 05, 06).

**- Product Affinity & Cross-Sell Analysis:** Discovering which products are commonly purchased together to identify cross-selling opportunities (Query 07).

**- Conversion Funnel Analysis:** Calculating the conversion rates from product view to add-to-cart and finally to purchase, pinpointing drop-off points in the customer journey at a product level (Query 08).

## Methodology
**- Tool:** Google BigQuery for data storage and processing.

**- Language:** Standard SQL.

**-Key Techniques:** The analysis heavily relies on advanced SQL techniques to handle the nested JSON-like structure of the Analytics data, including:

  + UNNEST to flatten repeated fields (like hits and products).

  + Common Table Expressions (CTEs) to structure complex queries logically.

  + Aggregation functions (SUM, COUNT, AVG) and conditional aggregation (COUNTIF).

  + Date functions for time-based analysis.
